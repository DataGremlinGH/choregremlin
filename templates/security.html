Safe Port Forwarding Guide

If you enable remote access, you'll need to forward port {{ net_port if net_port is defined else 8473 }} on your router to your Pi's IP address. Here's how to do it safely:

  1. Set up HTTPS first. Use Caddy or Nginx with Let's Encrypt (for a domain name) or DuckDNS (for a dynamic IP). Without HTTPS, your login details travel in plain text.
  2. Enable 2FA. This means even if someone guesses your password, they still can't get in without the code from your phone.
  3. Forward only the app port ({{ net_port if net_port is defined else 8473 }}) to the Pi. Don't forward any other ports.
  4. Reserve your Pi's IP in your router's DHCP settings so it doesn't change.
  5. Use a strong admin password. At least 12 characters with a mix of letters, numbers and symbols.
Risks of port forwarding: Opening a port to the internet means anyone in the world can try to access your app. HTTPS and 2FA make this safe, but if you're not sure, keep remote access off and use the app only on your home Wi-Fi.
